# UI UX Pro Max — Design Intelligence

## Typography
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display, light weight (300), large scale
- Body: Inter or DM Sans, 16px base, 1.6 line height
- Letter spacing: headings +0.08em, nav +0.15em uppercase

## Colors
- Dark luxury: #0a0a0a base, #c9a84c gold, #1a4a1a forest green
- Backgrounds layer: #0a0a0a → #111 → #1a1a1a for cards

## Spacing
- 8px grid: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px
- Section padding: 120px top/bottom desktop, 64px mobile

## Animation (Framer Motion)
- Entrance: fade up, y: 40→0, duration 0.7, ease easeOut
- Stagger children: 0.1s delay
- Hover: scale 1.02, transition 0.3s

## Rules
- Hero always fullscreen 100vh
- Every section needs a micro-interaction
- CTAs: solid gold primary, ghost outline secondary
